Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Popsel, export formats, and creator privacy.

What is Popsel?

Popsel is a high-performance pixel reveal web application. It transforms photos into popping-dot animation videos with customizable reveal physics, aesthetic color palettes, and instant 60 FPS video export.

How does the pixel reveal animation work?

Popsel samples pixel colors from your uploaded photo and procedurally calculates particle spawn delays based on your chosen pattern (Scatter, Sweep, Ripple, Spiral, Shadows First, or Contour Outlines).

Does Popsel upload my photos to any server?

No. Popsel is 100% client-side and serverless. All pixel processing, canvas rendering, and video encoding run locally inside your browser on your device.

What export formats and aspect ratios are supported?

Popsel exports smooth 60 FPS HD MP4 or WebM video reels, animated looping GIF stickers, and high-resolution PNG comparison stills across 9:16 Story, 1:1 Square, 4:5 Feed, and 16:9 Widescreen aspect ratios.

Who created Popsel?

Popsel was created and designed by Teja Priyan as an open-source, private, in-browser creative studio for creators, artists, and social media producers.

Is Popsel mobile and laptop friendly?

Yes. Popsel features an adaptive responsive layout optimized for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide desktop displays with full touch and keyboard shortcut support.
